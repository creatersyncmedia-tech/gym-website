About IronForge

Built for peoplewho show up.

Our story

Started in a warehouse.

IronForge was founded in 2015 with a simple goal: build a training environment where serious athletes and complete beginners could train side by side, and both would be better for it.

We started with one converted warehouse, a handful of racks and three coaches who believed a gym should feel like a team. Members brought friends, friends became regulars, and the floor got busier and louder in the best way.

A decade on, the equipment is better and the space is bigger, but the rule hasn't changed: show up, and we'll help you get somewhere.

Philosophy

Three rules we train by.

Train with purpose.

Every set has a reason. We program around your goal, not around what looks impressive.

Recover with intention.

Sleep, mobility and downtime are part of the plan, so we give them space in the building.

Improve every day.

Small, honest gains compound. We track them and celebrate them.

Mission and vision

Mission

Make strength accessible.

To give every member expert coaching, elite equipment and a supportive community, whatever their starting point.

Vision

The region's home for training.

To be the place people choose for a lifetime of health, where getting stronger is a habit and not a phase.
