Our coaching philosophy

How our coaches think.

Technique first.

Load only goes up when movement quality holds. We'd rather you lift 20 lb less and lift it well.

Coach the person.

Nobody has the same body, schedule or history. Programs bend around you, not the other way round.

Measure everything.

If it matters, we track it. Progress is proven with numbers, not guesses.
